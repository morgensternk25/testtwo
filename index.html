Study debrief

This study examines whether misleading post-event information changes recognition memory, and whether feedback after a selection influences confidence.

The original video always showed a STOP sign. Participants were randomly assigned to a question mentioning either a stop sign or a yield sign, and independently to either confirming (“Correct!”) or neutral (“Okay.”) feedback.

The STOP-sign image is the correct recognition response. The YIELD-sign image is the altered alternative. Feedback was assigned independently of actual accuracy.
